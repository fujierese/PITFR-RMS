## 4.2 Approval workflow and digital approval

**D8, the real order of stages (from the code):**
1. Requestor submits.
2. Venue Custodian confirms venue availability (`venue_status`).
3. Supply Office initial availability review (`supply_office_review_status`); for urgent requests it also validates the supporting document.
4. In parallel: Equipment Custodian(s) endorse their items (`equipment_status`), skipped when no equipment; and for Outsiders the IGP records payment (`igp_payment_status`).
5. Supply Office final approval.

## Decisions applied (Nov 18)

| Decision | Result |
|---|---|
| **D4** (REG-01, REG-05) | **Done (needs the migration).** Registration is open to students, faculty, staff and Outsiders. The chosen category only applies once verified; until then Outsider rules apply. **PIT email** (`PITFR_INSTITUTIONAL_EMAIL_DOMAINS`, default `pit.edu.ph`): verified automatically after the emailed code. **ID upload**: PIT ID, or any government ID for Outsiders (PhilSys, driver's license, passport, UMID, PRC, postal, voter's). JPG, PNG or PDF up to 5 MB, stored on the private disk. It can be uploaded at registration or later from Account Settings. The Supply Office reviews it under **Account Verification** (sidebar badge shows how many are waiting): it can approve, correcting the category if needed, or reject with a reason. The **image is deleted after either decision**; only the ID type, the result, who decided and when are kept. Every view of an ID is written to the audit log. **Final approval is blocked** on both final-approval paths until the requester is verified, and the request page explains why. Existing accounts and accounts the Supply Office creates count as verified. Migration `2026_11_18_000001_add_account_verification_to_users`; until it runs, the new fields are skipped. Test: `AccountVerificationTest`. |
| **D5** (APR-08) | **Kept as built:** a custodian who endorsed a request cannot also give its final approval (both final-approval paths). |
| **D3 mark** (FRM-01) | **Done.** The Create Request page no longer shows the decorative form header (PIT logo block, reference code, Rev. No.) or the TUV NORD mark; the page starts with its title and Section I. The printed form keeps the official header and the TUV NORD mark, as on the controlled template. Test: `RequestFormHeaderTest`. |
| **D7** (REG-08) | **Done.** Category and office or organization are separate. The **category** (student, faculty, staff, outsider) is chosen at registration, drives the rules, and only applies once verified (D4). The **office, department or organization** is a display field: registration suggests PIT offices, departments and active student organizations as you type, and still accepts any company or group name for Outsiders. The form says it does not change which rules apply. |
| **D2** (REQ-01, REQ-02) | **Done (needs the migration).** Each selected venue is a separate booking with its own schedule (already supported) and now its **own participant count** ("Participants at this venue", defaulting to the total). A venue whose capacity is below that count is **greyed out and can't be selected**, with the reason shown ("Capacity 50 — below the 80 participants at this venue"). Lowering the count, for a group split across venues, re-enables it. The server enforces the same rule; a single venue's count can't exceed the total. **Exception:** "Request a capacity exception" asks for a reason. The request is flagged, and at availability review the Supply Office must tick "Approve the capacity exception" (or mark it unavailable). Approver and time are recorded and added to the history. Tests: `VenueCapacityRulesTest`, updated `PerVenueSchedulingTest`. |
| **D6** (REG-06, PRV-01) | **Draft done; final wording waits on PIT.** Registration requires "I have read the Privacy Notice" (links to the notice and to RA 10173), and the acceptance time is stored in `users.privacy_accepted_at` (migration `2026_11_18_000003`).
